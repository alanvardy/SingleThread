# SingleThread

Your brain does one thing at a time — your list should too.

SingleThread shows one Apple Reminder at a time for calm, focused momentum.

---

SingleThread turns your Apple Reminders into a single, calm, focused list. Instead of an intimidating wall of everything you "should" do, you see one clear reminder at a time, in order, and you process it — complete, skip, or delete — before moving to the next.

## Why you'll love it

- **One at a time.** Your brain does one thing at a time — your task list should reflect that. Just one reminder fills the screen, so every task gets your full attention.
- **Order that makes sense.** Sort by priority, by due date (soonest first), or alphabetically. Pick whichever removes the most thinking.
- **No decision paralysis.** See the reminder, act, move on. Fast. Smooth. Calm.

## Everything you need, nothing you don't

SingleThread stays out of your way, but it's as full-featured — or as bare — as you want. Every capability can be switched on or off in one simple Settings screen:

- Complete, skip, or delete each reminder with a tap, a swipe, or the action buttons.
- Hide reminders from projects you don't want to see.
- Hide undated ("someday") reminders, or show them when you're ready.
- Show or hide reminder dates — keep the screen as minimal as you like.
- Add fast by voice: dictate a reminder, due date and recurrence included, from the microphone button (or hide it with one switch).
- Change how it looks: System, Light, or Dark appearance, plus larger text sizes across the whole interface.

## Thoughtful by design

- Works with your existing Apple Reminders — everything you've already captured stays right where it is. Two-way, in place.
- Calm visual design that gets out of the way. Big, legible text. Nothing flashing at you.
- Accessible — clear hit targets, complete descriptions and labels, and full support for Apple's accessibility features.
- Syncs across your devices: iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with a matching Apple Watch app and macOS widget. No account to create — your reminders stay in place.
