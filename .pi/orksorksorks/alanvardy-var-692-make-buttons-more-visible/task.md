# Task: Make buttons more visible (VAR-692)

When a background photo is displayed, the microphone and settings icons are
hard to see. Make these icons (and the Complete/Skip action buttons when
visible) readable against any background by giving them a contrasting outline
and fill: black on a dark/solid background and white in light display mode,
with an off-white treatment for the icon fills and background. The change is
entirely visual/styling-focused and applies to the iOS app controls.
