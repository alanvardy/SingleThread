# VAR-801 — Reminder tap on watch

Tapping the reminder card on the Apple Watch currently opens a confirmation
dialog offering Refresh and Delete. The goal is to make a tap on the card
simply refresh the reminder list directly, without showing that
Refresh/Delete menu. The scope is confined to the card-tap interaction; the
other delete/action entry points (action menu, skip-nudge banner, Complete
button) are not part of this change.
