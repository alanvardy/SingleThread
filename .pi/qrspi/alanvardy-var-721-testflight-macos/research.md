## Q5: Build/test automation coverage

### Findings

## Cross-Cutting Observations

- **One physical app target, three SDKs**: everything iOS/macOS shares the `SingleThread` target (`SUPPORTED_PLATFORMS "iphoneos iphonesimulator macosx"`, `SDKROOT auto`); platform divergence is expressed through (a) per-SDK `CODE_SIGN_ENTITLEMENTS`/`CODE_SIGN_IDENTITY`/`LD_RUNPATH` settings, (b) macOS-only sandbox/hardened-runtime settings, (c) `#if os(iOS)`/`#if os(macOS)` source guards, and (d) `platformFilter = ios` dropping the watch+widget from macOS builds (`project.pbxproj:591,597`).
- **The macOS build path is already fully wired and alive**: native (non-Catalyst) app for macOS 26.5, sandboxed, with entitlements, its own appearance bridge (`MacAppDelegate`), keyboard shortcuts, EventKit authorization via `requestFullAccessToReminders`, App Group defaults, and WidgetKit reload; built and unit-tested in CI (`mac-tests`) and via `make mac-build`/`mac-test`. All macOS verification runs `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO`.
- **Watch is the odd platform**: not in the App Group (no entitlements), everything forced to `.standard`, sync exclusively over WatchConnectivity, EventKit read-only. WatchConnectivity is the only iOS/macOS divergence that is also watch-positive (`#if os(iOS) || os(watchOS)`).
- **Dictation is the least-gated macOS risk surface**: Speech/AVFoundation stack compiles and runs on macOS with only `AVAudioSession` gated; conversely UserNotifications and all UIKit/UIApplication usage are 100% iOS-gated.
- **Persistence model is uniform across iOS+macOS**: one shared App Group suite for synced prefs + `.standard` for cosmetics; macOS differs only in that the iOS-gated `.standard` keys don't exist.
- **Freemium and purchase are recycled everywhere**: `canMutate` gate, `completionCount`, `isEntitled` payload — a consistent single-source `unlockProductID` with UI-test seams in both StoreKit (`testingWithEntitled:`) and seed JSON.
- **Build/test automation is Debug-and-test only**: every macOS step forces `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO`; no Release, archive, export, signing, notarization, or distribution step exists in any automation layer.
